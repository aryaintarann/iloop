# Hotel case study template

Internal template. Do not publish an empty case study or treat the website’s illustrative dashboard values as measured results.

## Property and scope

- Hotel/property name, permission to identify it, and authorised customer contact.
- Participating properties, channels, workflows, product configuration and staff responsibilities.
- Pilot/start date and any relevant changes in staffing, occupancy or operations.

## Measurement record

- Baseline period: exact start/end dates, timezone and reason for selecting the period.
- Comparison period: exact start/end dates, timezone and comparable operating conditions.
- Data source: system/report/export, owner, extraction date and location of the supporting records.
- Metric definitions: unit, numerator, denominator, inclusion/exclusion criteria and sample size.
- Method: baseline and comparison calculation, handling of missing data, deduplication and whether changes are absolute or relative.
- Results: verified values only; retain the calculation and source for each published number.
- Limitations: channel coverage, seasonality, occupancy/staffing changes, other concurrent changes and uncertainty. Do not attribute a change to iloop.id without evidence supporting that attribution.

## Story and publication approval

- Hotel’s original problem, actual setup and what staff observed.
- Quotes: exact wording, speaker’s verified name/role and explicit permission.
- Publication permission: approver, date, permitted name/logo/quotes/metrics, approved final text and restrictions.
- Privacy review: remove guest-identifying details and confirm permitted use of the underlying records.
- Evidence reviewer and final owner sign-off before adding a public page or sitemap entry.
