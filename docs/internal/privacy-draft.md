# Privacy notice draft

Internal working document. Do not publish or place in `src/pages/` or `public/`. This draft is incomplete and does not establish current data practices.

## Proposed notice structure

**Operator and contact.** iloop.id provides an AI hotel chatbot and guest messaging assistant. Product enquiries go to hello@iloop.id. Add the verified operator name, business address, privacy contact and the parties responsible for website enquiries and hotel guest conversations.

**Scope and information collected.** Describe the website, demo enquiries, pilot and hotel service separately. Confirm which contact details, messages, conversation history, hotel information, technical logs and account details each workflow processes. Confirm whether the hosting provider keeps access logs. Demo and waitlist links open the visitor’s email application; document what happens after an enquiry reaches the mailbox.

**Purposes and responsibilities.** Explain the confirmed purposes for each data category and the respective responsibilities of the operator and hotel. Confirm any legal basis, guest notices or permissions needed for the service. Do not assume that one notice covers every hotel’s processing.

**Providers and transfers.** List the actual hosting, email, messaging, AI and other data processors, their roles, processing locations and any transfer arrangements. Verify whether any provider uses conversations for model training. Do not publish a training-use promise before confirming the contracts and configuration.

**Retention and deletion.** Supply actual retention periods or criteria for enquiries, conversations, accounts, logs and backups. Describe how requests and account closure trigger deletion, who handles them, and any exceptions.

**Requests and safeguards.** Specify a verified request channel and procedure for access, correction and deletion. Describe only implemented access controls and security measures. Confirm the incident contact and notification process.

**Browser events and analytics.** The current website dispatches `iloop:cta-click` locally with action, placement and pathname. Its CTA handler does not store or send those details. No external analytics integration is part of this change. Review this section if a consumer of the event, analytics service or new cookie is added. The existing intro uses session storage for its per-tab display preference; account for this separately from analytics.

**Changes.** Add a real effective date and a confirmed process for notifying users of material changes when the notice is ready to publish.

## Information required before publication

- Verified operator identity, address and privacy contact.
- Data categories and purposes for website, email enquiries, pilot and guest messaging.
- Operator/hotel responsibilities and applicable requirements.
- Actual processors, subprocessors, locations, transfers and AI provider data-use terms.
- Retention periods, backup handling and deletion procedure.
- Request handling, safeguards, incident procedure and browser storage inventory.
- Owner approval of the completed notice and effective date.
