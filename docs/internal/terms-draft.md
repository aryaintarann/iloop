# Terms of service draft

Internal working document. Do not publish or place in `src/pages/` or `public/`. Commercial and service terms remain unconfirmed.

## Proposed terms structure

**Parties and acceptance.** Add the verified operator identity, address and authorised customer role. Confirm how a customer accepts terms and which agreement governs a demo, pilot or paid service. Product contact: hello@iloop.id.

**Service scope.** Describe the agreed guest messaging channels, hotel information, staff handover, reporting and property scope. List integrations only after checking the customer’s actual systems. Record what setup, support and third-party accounts each party supplies.

**Hotel responsibilities.** Agree how the hotel supplies and updates rooms, policies, services and FAQs, manages staff access and reviews guest conversations. Confirm responsibilities for guest notices, permissions and use of channel accounts.

**Pilot, charges and service levels.** Supply the actual pilot period, scope, exit process, prices, billing, taxes, support hours and any agreed service levels. Do not infer free access, guaranteed outcomes or universal integration compatibility from the website.

**Data and providers.** Link the final privacy notice and any agreed data-processing terms. Confirm operator/hotel roles, providers, retention, exports and deletion after termination. Document third-party dependencies and responsibilities.

**Use and ownership.** Agree permitted use, prohibited conduct, customer content rights and rights to the software and outputs. Confirm how inappropriate use or account compromise is handled.

**Ending the service and disputes.** Supply verified suspension and termination conditions, notice periods, refund/cancellation terms, liability terms, governing law and dispute process. Do not select a jurisdiction or legal entity without owner confirmation.

**Changes.** Add an approved effective date, version and change-notification procedure.

## Information required before publication

- Operator identity and customer contracting/acceptance process.
- Final service scope, pilot conditions, pricing, support and service levels.
- Hotel responsibilities, channel prerequisites and approved integrations.
- Actual processors and data-processing agreement, retention and deletion/export terms.
- Ownership, acceptable use, suspension, termination, refunds and liability terms.
- Governing law, disputes, owner approval and effective date.
